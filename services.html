Our Classes & Services

Find the Right Practice for You

Class Types

Gentle Yoga

A slow-paced class focusing on foundational postures, breathwork, and accessibility. Ideal for beginners, older adults, or anyone recovering from injury.

Vinyasa Flow

A dynamic, breath-to-movement practice designed to build strength and flexibility. Perfect for busy professionals looking to release physical and mental tension.

Restorative Yoga

A deeply relaxing practice using props (bolsters, blankets) to support the body in long-held, comfortable poses. A perfect way to end a stressful week.

Private Sessions

Work one-on-one with an instructor to tailor a practice specifically for your body, goals, and experience level. Contact us for scheduling.
